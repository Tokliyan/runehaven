# RUNEHAVEN — GAME DESIGN BIBLE
Definitive Edition | Confidential | Peer Review Draft
One world. One history. Shared by all.

## THE WORLD
Name: RuneHaven
Genre: 2D Persistent Open World Sandbox Survival RPG
Setting: A vast magical fantasy world of ancient ruins, enchanted forests, volcanic mountains, underwater caves, sacred meadows and stormy peaks
Generation: Single procedurally generated world — one world shared by all players
Size: Very large — bases genuinely hard to find by design
Persistence: Server runs 24/7, never stops even when players are offline
Updates: Periodically bring new content, areas, pets and items into the single living world

## TUTORIAL & NAVIGATION
Tutorial Grounds — A small guided area within the Spawn Safe Zone teaching movement, basic combat, and pet taming before players enter the open world
Minimap / Compass — Shows general direction only, does not reveal exact base locations, helps with orientation in the large world
Player Level Display — Each player's level is shown above their character, next to their username, visible to everyone

## BIOMES & LOCATIONS
Spawn Safe Zone — Protected, no PvP, new player starting point
Forests — Tree sprites, wolves, boars, basic resources
Enchanted Forest — Unicorns at night, rare herbs, magic materials
Dark Forest — Shadowfox spawns at night
Plains — Wind sprites, boars, open PvP zones
Mountains — Stone sprites, griffins, iron ore, storm dragons at peaks
Underground Caves — Golems, iron, runic stone, fire dragons in lava caves
Underwater Caves — Water dragons, rare aquatic resources
Ruins — Young golems, rare loot, dungeon entrances
Dungeons — Demon Knights, dragonsteel drops, extreme danger
Sacred Meadow — Lightfox chance spawn at dawn only
Volcano — Elder Drake world boss, dragonsteel ore
The Shattered Peaks — Storm Dragon territory, extreme danger
The Abyssal Hollow — Deepest point in world, Shadow Dragon territory
The Sunforge Caldera — Beyond the volcano, blinding heat, rare dragonsteel
Other Safe Zones — Scattered neutral trading and resting areas

## LANDMARK STRUCTURES
The Eternal Tower — Centrepiece of the world, visible from anywhere, Golden Orb sits at the top, legendary loot throughout
The Ruined Colosseum — PvP arena, open player duels
The Ancient Forge — Only place to smelt dragonsteel weapons
The Grand Bazaar — Player trading hub inside a safe zone, item-for-item trading only, no currency system, trades happen at players' own risk
The Beastmaster Shrine — Boosts pet taming success rate when activated
The Oracle — NPC that hints at rare pet spawn locations, cannot hint at Elder Trio locations

## CLASSES
All classes can tame all pets. Beastmaster has a higher success rate.
Reclassing resets all XP and levels but keeps inventory, base and pets.
Ranger — Ranged combat, scouting, stealth | Best weapons: Bows, crossbows, daggers
Knight — Melee tank, frontline fighter | Best weapons: Swords, axes, shields
Mystic — Magic attacks, buffs, area damage | Best weapons: Staves, runic weapons
Beastmaster — Pet capturing bonus, pet stat buffs | Best weapons: Spears, daggers
Architect — Faster building, stronger structures, resource generation | Best weapons: Axes, shields

## BASES
Built anywhere outside safe zones — location is your primary defence
Anyone who finds your base can walk straight in and take everything
Store and passively generate resources over time
Can be fully raided and destroyed
Hide your base well — the world is vast, obscurity is your best protection
Higher material tiers are your backup defence if found
All teaming is organised outside the game — Discord, group chats, in person

### Material Tiers
Tier 1 — Wood — Low durability — Chop trees
Tier 2 — Stone — Medium durability — Mine rocks
Tier 3 — Iron — High durability — Mine and smelt
Tier 4 — Runic Stone — Very high durability — Deep caves and ruins
Tier 5 — Dragonsteel — Near indestructible — Kill adult wild dragons, Demon Knights, or Elder Drake

## MOUNTABLE PETS
Certain pets can be ridden for faster traversal: Stag, Griffin, Crystal Golem, Water Dragon, Fire Dragon, Storm Dragon, Shadow Dragon, Shadowfox, Lightfox

## DRAGONSTEEL — ACQUISITION & STAKES
The rarest and most powerful material in RuneHaven. Only obtained by:
Killing an adult wild dragon in the world
Killing a Demon Knight in a dungeon
Defeating the Elder Drake world boss at the volcano
Killing another player's tamed dragon — owning a dragon is both a power and a permanent target on your back
Dragonsteel gear and weapons emit a faint purple aura, making owners instantly recognisable in the world — a visible mark of how dangerous, and targetable, they are

## MOBS
Mobs are obstacles and loot sources, not the focus. RuneHaven is primarily player vs player, building and exploration.
Goblin — Forests, plains — Easy — Basic loot
Bandit — Plains, ruins — Easy — Basic weapons
Troll — Mountains — Medium — Stone, iron
Dark Wraith — Dark forest, dungeons — Medium — Runic materials
Adult Golem — Ruins — Hard — Runic stone
Sea Serpent — Underwater — Hard — Rare aquatic loot
Demon Knight — Deep dungeons — Very Hard — Dragonsteel
Elder Drake — Volcano, World Boss — Boss tier — Dragonsteel, legendary loot

## WORLD EVENTS
Blood Moon — Occurs roughly every 12 day cycles. Mobs become stronger and more aggressive, and rare pet spawn chances increase significantly for the duration
Meteor Shower — Occurs randomly with no fixed pattern. Rare ore drops appear at random locations across the map, causing a scramble for players to reach them first

## CRAFTING & SMELTING
Raw materials gathered and smelted into weapons and building materials
Basic smelting done at a player's own base forge
Dragonsteel smelting requires The Ancient Forge landmark only
Iron tier — Iron Sword, Shortbow, Crossbow, Hatchet, Apprentice Staff, Iron Dagger, Iron Spear, Iron Shield
Runic tier — Runic Blade, Runic Crossbow, Longbow, Runic Axe, Mystic Staff, Venomblade, Runic Lance, Runic Shield
Dragonsteel tier — Dragonsteel Sword, Dragonsteel Bow, Dragonsteel Axe, Elder Runestaff, Shadow Dagger, Dragonsteel Lance, Dragonsteel Shield

## PLAYER CUSTOMISATION
Username chosen at first login
All cosmetics earned through gameplay only, never purchased
Hats — Cap, Hood, Crown, Witch Hat, Knight Helm, Feathered Hat
Cloaks — Colour variants, unlocked through gameplay
Weapon Skins — Cosmetic only, no stat changes
Pet Accessories — Collars, ribbons, small cosmetic items

## DEATH & RESPAWN
Players drop all items on death
No death marker — dropped items simply lie where the player died, untracked
Respawn occurs at the Spawn Safe Zone after a 10 minute wait

## TEAMING & ALLIANCES
No formal guild or clan system exists in the game. All alliances and teaming are organised outside of RuneHaven — Discord, group chats, in person. Players can fight together, share base locations, or coordinate raids entirely on their own terms. This keeps the in-game world raw and unpredictable — anyone could be an enemy or an ally.

## THE WORLD-ENDING EVENT (Admin Only — Secret)
**This section must never be hinted at, documented publicly, or referenced by the Oracle. Players should discover this purely through emergent gameplay, not through patch notes or community knowledge.**

If the Golem Elder and Dragon Elder are ever in the same location and enter combat — whether through player conflict or accident:
The Unicorn Elder senses it anywhere in the world
It travels to The Beastmaster Shrine and sacrifices itself
A world reset is triggered — new world seed generated, all bases and progress wiped, all three Elders lost
Incredibly hard to trigger accidentally
Could theoretically be weaponised by players as a last resort if they ever discover it
This will be the most legendary moment in the server's history if it ever happens

## ADMIN — FULL DETAILS (Admin Only)
One special admin account, controlled by the project owner
Exclusive cosmetics: Duskfox Elder pet (one of a kind, admin exclusive), unique crown and cloak
Full world moderation — ban, kick, mute players
Ability to promote other players to admin via Supabase (role column: player → admin)
Player management — view inventories, reset progress, teleport players
World management — view all base locations, force day/night cycle, trigger or cancel world events (Blood Moon, Meteor Shower) manually
Invisible mode — observe players without being seen
Full visibility into whether the Golem Elder and Dragon Elder are ever in proximity to each other, so the admin can monitor for the world-ending event before it happens
Server-side controls for deploying updates and new content into the single living world
